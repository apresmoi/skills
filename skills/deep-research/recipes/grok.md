# Grok specifics

- Start at `https://grok.com/` or the remembered `Deep research` project.
  A project's composer keeps the model menu, so research depth is still
  verified before sending.
- **Model menu** `Auto · Fast · Expert · Build · Heavy · Imagine`. There is
  no separate DeepSearch toggle: **Expert** is the research tier (default),
  **Heavy** the slower multi-agent one. The runner verifies the requested
  label is selected; a visible composer alone is not enough.
- The differentiator is **X search**: Grok reads public posts, replies,
  quote posts, and profiles directly. That makes it the site for anything
  about how a story moved, who said what first, and what an account has
  said over time. See `recipes/research/`.
- What X search does not give you, so the recipes make Grok say so
  explicitly:
  - private or deleted posts, and posts from accounts that block search;
  - reliable view or impression counts for older posts;
  - a true "earliest": it returns the earliest its index surfaces, not a
    guarantee. Origin claims always get a manual check on the actual posts.
- Wait: 10 to 30 minutes at Expert, longer at Heavy. Heavy is worth it for
  timelines spanning many accounts; Expert for single-claim checks.
